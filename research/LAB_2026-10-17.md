# OG X research lab: 17 Oct 2026

What was tested, how, and what it means for OG X. Code: `tools/lab/` (C simulator `sim.c`, `lab.py`, one script per study). Data: Binance USDT perpetuals, 15m → 45m, Oct 2024 – Oct 2026, 165 coins (96 with history from Oct 2024). Costs everywhere unless stated: **fees 0.10 % round trip + slippage 0.05 % per side**. Periods: 2024–25 = Oct 2024 – Dec 2025, 2026 = Jan – Oct 2026.

## Headline findings
1. **The edge survives real costs.** At 0.10 % fees + 0.05–0.10 % slippage per side, the 41 presets' median drops from +195 % to about +130–140 % (2 years), and 39–40 of 41 stay profitable in 2026. Fast presets (60 bars) are the most sensitive. Above 0.2 % slippage per side, the median halves.
2. **Hold to stop or next signal is still the best exit for profit.** Breakeven stops, % and ATR trailing stops, and time stops all cost more than they save. One useful exception: **take 50 % at +8 %** → 2-year median +140 % vs +142 %, median worst drawdown 35 % vs 45 %.
3. **Past results per coin don't predict future results.** Coin/strategy pairs that were profitable in both Oct 2024 – Jun 2025 and Jul – Dec 2025 were profitable in 2026 **35 %** of the time, against 40 % for all pairs (reference engine: 37 % vs 43 %; rank correlation of earlier vs later results 0.19). Selecting on 2024–25 alone: 31 % vs 41 %.
   - **Consequence:** the presets' 2026 numbers and the ★ ratings are in-sample (2026 was used to choose them). The 41-preset portfolio's +278 % / 14 % DD is an upper bound, not an expectation.
4. **The raw engine on coins chosen without hindsight is roughly break-even.** All 96 coins with full history, equal weight, real costs: ETH strategy −6 % over 2 years (DD 33 %), v.5 −26 %, v2.2 −22 %. Median coin loses; the mean is slightly positive because a few runners are huge (trend-following's fat right tail).
5. **What genuinely helps, in both periods and across coins:**
   - **Trend filter**: take Longs only when the coin is above its 15-day EMA *and* BTC is above its own; Shorts only when both are below. It beats the unfiltered version on ~70 % of coin/strategy pairs in both periods; coins profitable in both periods 46 → 74; basket drawdown down 8–10 points. On the 41 presets: about neutral for profit, a little lower drawdown (the coin-trend part alone: median +153 % vs +142 %, 2026 +54 % vs +47 %).
   - **~20-day channel (640 bars on 45m)** is the best channel for a basket; the timeframe itself (45m / 2h / 4h) barely matters. 1D is a bit worse.
   - **Volatility sizing** (position size ∝ 1 / recent ATR %, clipped 0.2–3×): better in every configuration tested, Sharpe +0.2–0.3.
   - **Half size after a big win (> +10 %)**: the next trade after a big win is weaker in both periods; at equal average exposure the median improves 76 → 87 % (2024–25) and 47 → 57 % (2026).
   - **Cross-sectional momentum** (only trade coins in the top/bottom third by 30-day return) + trend filter: lower returns but **drawdown about 11 %**, profitable in both periods.
6. **Best honest basket found** (all 96 coins, no hindsight): 640-bar channel, 12 % stop, trend filter, volatility sizing → **+44 % over 2 years, max DD 18.5 %, Sharpe 1.0, positive in both periods (+11.5 % / +27.3 %)**. Top-20 liquid coins (picked from Oct–Dec 2024 volume): +88 %, DD 25 %, Sharpe 1.15, but almost all of it in 2024–25.
7. **Didn't help:** volatility-scaled (ATR) stops vs fixed %, a universal setting for all coins, multi-lookback ensembles (10/20/40/60 days), volume and squeeze filters on presets, **DCA** (−7 / −15 / −25 % adds with a −30 % stop: per unit of capital the 2-year median falls from +76 % to +12 %, return per unit of drawdown 1.7 → 0.8).

## Caveats
- Two years of data, one market cycle. 2025 was a weak year for altcoins.
- The coin list is today's top-volume coins, which flatters (survivorship bias); delisted coins are missing.
- The basket grid (channel length × stop × filter) was itself searched; the 20-day channel stood out clearly from its neighbours, but treat the exact numbers as optimistic.
- Intrabar fills and same-bar stop/TP ordering are approximations on 45m bars.

## Literature check
- Barbon et al., *Catching Crypto Trends* (2025): an ensemble of Donchian breakouts on the 20 most liquid coins with volatility sizing, survivorship-free data since 2015, net Sharpe > 1.5. Our data confirms the volatility sizing and liquid-universe parts; the ensemble did not help on Oct 2024 – Oct 2026. https://abarbon.com/papers/catching-crypto-trends
- *Momentum Trading in Cryptocurrencies: Time-Series vs Cross-Sectional* (2025): time-series momentum beat cross-sectional on a risk-adjusted basis on 8 major coins; cross-sectional suffered from high correlation (55 % DD). https://www.journals.vu.lt/BATP/en/article/view/44540
